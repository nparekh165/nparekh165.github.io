Shockback Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Developer: Shockback Team

This Privacy Policy explains how Shockback ("the game", "we", "us"), developed by the Shockback Team, handles information when you play the game on Android. By playing, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information inside the game.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, characters, powers, settings and daily rewards) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Android may back up this save file to your own Google account (Android Backup) so it can be restored if you reinstall the game; this backup is controlled by Google and by your device settings. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also stored in your Google Play Games account (Saved Games) so you can continue on another phone; Google provides your Play Games player name and ID to the game. We do not run our own servers and never see this data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads (for example revive, double coins or free coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. On first launch you can choose personalized or non-personalized ads, and you can change this at any time in Settings. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation of the purchase so the game can deliver the item. VIP subscriptions (SHOCK PLUS / SHOCK ELITE) renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions); the game checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your daily streak is about to end). They are created locally on your phone; no notification server is used. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android settings.

6. Permissions

Internet: to load ads, check purchases and subscriptions with Google Play, and sync Play Games cloud saves. Vibrate: for haptic feedback during play. Notifications: for optional daily reminders. Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

7. Information you send us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the details you choose to share (such as your device model and Android version). We use them only to answer you and improve the game, and we do not sell them.

8. Children

Shockback is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data (Settings > Apps > Shockback > Storage > Clear data). Android Backup copies can be deleted in your Google account's backup settings, and Play Games cloud saves in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete Play Games account & data). Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you; ask us to delete them at any time.

10. Security

We use the protections provided by Android and Google Play. No method of storing or sending data is 100% secure, but we do not collect personal data on our own servers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will appear in the game and on our privacy policy page, with a new effective date.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: shockback.support@gmail.com
